The business problem

Commitments were spread across chats, email, and meetings. Managers needed one dependable list, clear ownership, and a consistent way to follow up.

The design decision

Keep the PM in control. Use a strict five-field format as the handoff between AI output and automation. Send each owner one reminder organized by urgency.

Value to measure

Track review time, owner/date completeness, overdue rate, and status-update latency. No quantified business results are claimed in this reconstruction.
